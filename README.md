# RadioCar

A browser-based Spotify player inspired by classic car stereos, with a digital display, physical-style controls, and customizable lighting.

[Open RadioCar](https://mateusfrmacedo.github.io/radiocar/)

## Features

- Browse your Spotify playlists and liked songs.
- Play, pause, skip tracks, adjust volume, and seek within a song.
- Resume your Spotify session with its playback position and queue. If the session has expired, play the most recent track from your history from the beginning.
- Toggle shuffle and repeat.
- View the current song, artist, and playback progress on the LCD display.
- Customize the theme, lighting color, and interface size.
- Switch between screen animations and an animated equalizer.
- Enable **MIC EQ** to make the equalizer react to sound captured by your microphone.
- Open a compact **Picture-in-Picture** player in supported browsers.
- Share the RadioCar website with the share button.

## Getting started

Open the site, click the Spotify button, and connect your account. Select a song from your library or press Play to resume your session.

Browser playback requires **Spotify Premium**. MIC EQ requires microphone permission. Reconnect Spotify if prompted to grant access to collaborative playlists and listening history.

Built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and the Spotify Web Playback SDK.
